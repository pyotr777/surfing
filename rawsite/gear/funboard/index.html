Funboard

A stable bridge between a longboard and a shortboard — ideal for building real turns.

A funboard sits between a longboard and a shortboard: wide, buoyant, with a rounded outline and a 2+1 fin setup. It catches smaller waves early and paddles with much of the ease of a longboard.

What separates it from a longboard is that it will still turn. The shorter rail line lets you angle down the face and come back to it, which is the actual skill that has to develop between standing up and surfing. It gives you room to learn that movement without locking you into a longboard style.

Kugenuma is the obvious place for one. It is the classic progression board: forgiving in the small surf that makes up most of the calendar, and still useful when the first proper swells of the season arrive.
