What to wear in Chiba and Shonan

One table, two coasts, twelve months.

The areas covered here sit in a similar latitude band, but the water still changes dramatically with the season. In July and August, a 2 mm spring suit is usually enough; in winter the table points to 5 mm and the added warmth of a hood, boots and gloves.

Chiba and Shonan follow the same broad rhythm. Chiba's exposed Pacific beaches cool a little earlier in spring; by late summer both regions are warm, even as typhoon swell begins to arrive.

Reading the grid

Read across any row for a yearly gear plan. The numbers are thickness in millimetres, rather than a guarantee: wind, session length and your own cold tolerance can change what feels right.

The practical split is early summer through autumn, when 2–3 mm is common, and the winter half of the year, when the water calls for 4–5 mm. Check the actual forecast and local conditions before deciding.

The grid is generated from csv/wetsuit.csv during the build, including the colour of every cell — the shade is chosen from the number in the cell, so the pattern cannot drift out of step with the data.
