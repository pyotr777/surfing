Longboard

A family of boards built to make the most of trim, glide and small clean waves.

A longboard is not one fixed 9′2″ design but a family of boards. Their shared trait is a long rail line: it lets a board enter early, carry trim and connect the soft sections that stop a shorter shape. Within that family, length, outline, rocker and volume change the feeling completely.

A classic outline is made for steady trim; a noserider puts more area under the front foot; a performance longboard turns faster; and a glider stretches the rail line for effortless distance. These are different ways to use the same small, clean waves around Chiba and Shonan — not a ladder of ability.

More length also means more responsibility. A longboard takes time to redirect, is harder to manage through shorebreak, and carries real force in a crowded line-up. Choose a shape that suits the day, and leave extra room around other surfers.
