Gear

From a short fish to a longboard, a board’s shape, volume and length decide how it paddles, gathers speed and turns.

Boards

There is no universal board: a wide, high-volume fish gathers speed in weak surf, while a longboard gets in early. The ranges by each name are guides, not rigid rules.

Fins

Fins give a board its hold and direction. Choose a setup that suits the board, the waves and its fin boxes, then make sure every fin is fitted securely before you paddle out.

Leash

A leash is safety equipment. Use one made for the board, inspect the cord, rail saver and swivel before each session, and replace it when it is worn or damaged.

Wetsuit

Water temperature, wind and time in the sea decide what to wear. See the wetsuit guide by month and region.
