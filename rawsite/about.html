About Surf Japan

Surf Japan is an independent guide to Japan’s coast, boards and waves, made by surfers who enjoy understanding a place as much as riding it.

Built with Panehe

Panehe is a small static-site generator for HTML with build-time Python inserts. It keeps prose, templates and the small calculations behind maps, cards and diagrams close together, without adding a heavy framework.

Made by surfers

Surf Japan is made by surfers who believe surfing is about more than riding waves—it's about understanding the waves, spots, and gear themselves. The ocean belongs to everyone, from beginners to professionals, and there's something here for all skill levels. The aim is to make surfing interesting, accessible, and enjoyable.

Non-commercial and evolving

Surf Japan is developed on a non-commercial basis. Information is added gradually, and we don't keep to strict timelines. The project grows at its own pace, shaped by the people who care about it.
