About Surf Japan

Surf Japan is an independent guide to Japan’s coast, boards and waves, made by surfers who enjoy understanding a place as much as riding it.

Built with Panehe

Panehe is a small static-site generator for HTML with build-time Python inserts. It keeps prose, templates and the small calculations behind maps, cards and diagrams close together, without adding a heavy framework.

Made by surfers

We are surfers, not a commercial guidebook or a forecasting service. The site grows slowly as we learn a coast, revisit a break and find information worth checking against local conditions. We aim to make the context around a session clearer: how a beach faces a swell, what a board is designed to do, and why a wave changes on its way to shore.

Non-commercial and evolving

Surf Japan is developed on a non-commercial basis. There are no paid rankings or sponsored spot listings. Conditions can change quickly, and every description is only a starting point: check the forecast, tides, access and the beach itself before going in.
