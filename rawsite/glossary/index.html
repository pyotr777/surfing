Surf glossary

The vocabulary of waves, boards, conditions and riding — arranged as a set of connected classifications rather than one long A–Z list.

Open a classification, then a subgroup, to browse its terms and concise definitions. Some terms belong in more than one part of the surf world; this first edition gives each term one clear home and links established board guides where they already exist.

How it is organised

Classifications sort related terms by meaning. English is the canonical language; Russian and Japanese names will be added as localised terms and aliases.

There are no unclassified terms in this edition. New terms will be added to the relevant branch rather than collected in a separate catch-all list.
