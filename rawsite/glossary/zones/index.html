Zones in the water

Outside, break, inside and line-up on a beach break.

1. Outside
Outside is the water beyond the main breaking area, farther from shore. The swell is still unbroken here, so approaching sets can often be seen before they reach the surfers.
2. Break
The break is the zone where incoming waves steepen and start to collapse over shallow water. At a beach break, shifting sandbars can make different peaks break at different distances from shore.
3. Inside
The inside lies shoreward of the main line-up and breaking area. Broken waves run through it as whitewater, while some reform and break again closer to the beach.
4. Line-up
The line-up is where surfers wait near the outer edge of the break and position themselves for incoming waves. It is an area, not a straight row: surfers move between peaks as the sets and conditions change.
