Kisakihama

The warm end of the archipelago, and the closest thing Japan has to a sure thing.

Miyazaki occupies the southeast shoulder of Kyushu, angled into the Pacific in a way that catches almost everything. Kisakihama is its benchmark: solid beach break peaks with a swell window that runs from northeast round through east to south, which is an unusually wide target for one beach.

The result is consistency rather than perfection. Long, smooth rides and a lineup with room in it are the everyday experience here, which is why surfers who have run out of patience with Chiba crowds end up moving south. Typhoon swell in late summer is when it gets properly good; the rest of the year it simply keeps working.

Warmth is the other draw. The water is around 28 °C in August and still 18 °C in February — a spring suit for half the year and never more than 4 mm. Two thousand kilometres north, at Shioya, February water is 8 °C. Same country, same month, ten degrees of difference.
