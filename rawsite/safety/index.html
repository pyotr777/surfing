Safety

A safer session starts before you enter the water: look at the sea, know your limits, and make a plan that leaves room to turn back.

Before you paddle out

  • Check the forecast and the conditions you can actually see from the beach.
  • Consider the waves, current and wind together — and whether they suit your experience.
  • Follow lifeguard advice, signs and local rules.
  • Do not force a session alone or beyond your comfort zone.
