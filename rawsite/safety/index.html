Safety

A safer session starts before you enter the water: check the forecast, assess the sea, and decide whether the surf is safe for your experience and physical condition.

Before entering the water

  • Check the weather forecast, especially for sudden changes in conditions or hazardous weather. Avoid surfing after heavy rain Arnold et al. 2017.
  • Assess the waves: their size, frequency and breaking direction. Find the safest route to paddle out.
  • Follow advice from experienced surfers, signs and local rules.

In the water

  • Do not hold your board parallel to the waves.
  • Always use a leash.
  • Do not let go of your board when people are behind you.
